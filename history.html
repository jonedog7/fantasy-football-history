The League Begins

The Kevin Durant League is established as a four-team fantasy football league.

2014

The league expands from four teams to six managers.

2016

Eight-Team Era

The league expands again, reaching eight teams for the first time.

2021

Ten-Team League

The league grows to ten teams and begins a new era of competition.

2023

The 12-Team Era

The league expands to its current 12-team format.

2026
